Frequently asked

How far ahead should we book?+

We recommend booking 3 to 4 weeks ahead, and 6 to 8 weeks for weddings and large events. For shorter notice, send a request and we’ll tell you what we can do.

Where do you deliver?+

We serve the greater metro area. Delivery fees depend on distance, and we’ll confirm the exact amount in your quote.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?+

Yes. We offer vegan and fasting-friendly dishes and can adjust the spice level. Tell us about any allergies in your request and we’ll plan around them.

How do deposits work?+

A 30% deposit secures your date, with the balance due one week before your event. The details are in your quote.
