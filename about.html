Our story

Wossenyelesh Catering began at a family table, where Sunday meals meant one big shared platter, plenty of injera and a pot of coffee that took all afternoon.

Today we bring that same spirit to weddings, offices and community gatherings. Every stew is simmered slowly with spice blends we mix ourselves, and every platter is made to be shared by hand.
